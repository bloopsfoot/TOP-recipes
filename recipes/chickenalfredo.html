Gabriel's Crazy Chicken Alfredo Recipe

I made this, and it was really good. But, I didn't have enough sauce for the amount of pasta I used so the pasta was a little dry. But, this recipe should have accounted for that as I doubled the alfredo sauce needed! (if you have any problems, don't ask me)

Ingredients:

  • 2 Jars of Alfredo Sauce
  • 1 Box of Pasta (preferrably fettucine)
  • 1 Chicken Breast
  • Spices (whatever you use to make you chicken good, I used paprika, salt, petter, garlic powder, and maybe more)
  • Cooking Oil

Directions:

  1. Get a pan, put it on the stove around medium-low heat and let the pan warm up for around 4-5 minutes.
  2. In a bowl, put your chicken breast in and whatever seasonings you have, be bold with it!
  3. Put some oil on the pan, doesn't need to be a lot, and fry your chicken breast on both sides for around 5 minutes, or until they reach a nice browning on the skin.
  4. In a pot, boil water and cook your pasta to al dente.
  5. Once the pasta reaches al dente, drain the water and lower the heat.
  6. Add in the jars of Alfredo Sauce into the pot of pasta and let it simmer for a minute or two.
  7. Take the pot of alfredo pasta off of the heat and into a container, then cut the chicken however you like.
  8. If you want parmesan cheese on it, now is the time to sprinkle it on. Now you are ready to serve!
