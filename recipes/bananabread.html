Mother's Beautiful Banana Bread Recipe

My mother makes fantastic banana bread, mind you. And I'm leaking this information for free on the world wide web so listen up. It is great, it is amazing, and no one is allowed to criticize it, for the baked good is perfect. It's banana bread, plain and simple.

Ingredients:

  • 3 to 4 Ripe Bananas
  • 1 and a quarter cups of All Purpose Flour
  • 3 quarters cup of Cooking Oil (Olive Oil/Avocado Oil)
  • Half a teaspoon of Salt
  • 3 quarters cup of White Sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of Vanilla Extract
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 teaspoon of Baking Soda

Directions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 340 degrees Farenheit
  2. In a bowl, mash the bananas, beat the eggs, and mix all of that with oil and the vanilla extract.
  3. In another bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking soda, and any solid toppings you would like to add.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients into the bowl with the wet ingredients until all the ingredients are incorporated well
  5. Grease your loaf pan, then pour the mixture into the pan
  6. Put the pan in the oven preheated to 340 degrees Farenheit for 50 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean when the bread is poked.
  7. Take the pan out of the oven and let it rest, and you're ready to serve!
